CeylonGems — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

CeylonGems ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile marketplace that connects gemstone buyers and sellers. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address — for account creation and login
  • Photos and videos you upload — stored via Cloudinary
  • Listing data — gem type, carats, price, location, description, WhatsApp number
  • Device information — collected automatically by Firebase
  • Usage data — app interactions, crash logs

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your listings to other users
  • To allow buyers to contact you via WhatsApp
  • To send you notifications about new gems (if enabled)
  • To improve the app and prevent abuse

4. Information Sharing

Your listing information (title, photos, description, location, WhatsApp number) is visible to all app users. Your email is not shared publicly.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, notifications
  • Cloudinary — image hosting
  • WhatsApp — external contact (you control)

These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your listings at any time. To delete your entire account, contact us.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Firebase and Cloudinary provide industry-standard security.

8. Children's Privacy

CeylonGems is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app means acceptance of changes.
