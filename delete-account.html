Delete Your CeylonGems Account

Last updated: October 2026

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open CeylonGems
  2. Sign in to your account
  3. Tap your Profile tab
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  5. Type DELETE to confirm

Your account, listings, and uploaded images are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email wajira4u@gmail.com from your registered email address with subject Delete Account. We will process the deletion within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, password)
  • All gemstone listings you posted
  • All images you uploaded (removed from Cloudinary)
  • Your favorites and preferences
  • Your notification settings

What May Be Retained

Anonymized transaction or safety logs may be kept for legal compliance for up to 12 months.
